Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

This app and website (“Spiritual Message”, video.spiritualmessage.org) are a free, non-commercial service by CPS Global to help people study the teachings of Maulana Wahiduddin Khan. There is no paywall and nothing is sold.

What we collect

  • Search queries: the words you search are logged (without any personal identifier) so we can see which topics people look for and which searches return nothing. This helps us improve the library. We do not build a profile of you.
  • Basic usage analytics: anonymous page-view counts via a self-hosted analytics tool. No cross-site tracking, no advertising cookies.
  • Account data (only if you sign in): if you have an operator/uploader login, we store your username and session cookie so the app knows you are signed in. Most visitors never sign in.

We do not ask for your name, phone number, or location, and we do not sell or share any data with advertisers.

YouTube videos

Lectures are played using the embedded YouTube player. When a video loads, YouTube and Google may set cookies and collect data (such as your IP address and device information) under their own policies — this happens directly between your device and Google, not through us. By watching a video you are also subject to:

  • YouTube Terms of Service
  • Google Privacy Policy

Audio uploads

If you use the upload feature to generate a transcript, your audio file is sent to a speech-to-text service for processing and then stored so the transcript can be displayed. Do not upload anything you are not authorised to share.

Children

This service is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

You can clear the app’s stored data at any time from your browser or phone settings. Search logs are anonymous and cannot be linked back to you to delete individually.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email info@spiritualmessage.org.
